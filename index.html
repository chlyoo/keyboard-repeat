Keyboard Repeat

A Mac menu bar utility that sets the repeat delay and speed of each key separately.

Support

Questions, bug reports and feature requests: open an issue at github.com/chlyoo/keyboard-repeat/issues. Include your macOS version and the key or setting involved.

If keys do not repeat, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and make sure Keyboard Repeat is allowed under both Input Monitoring and Accessibility, then quit and reopen the app.

Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Keyboard Repeat does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The app makes no network connections. It has no account, analytics, advertising or crash reporting.
  • Key presses and mouse clicks are read only to repeat the keys you configured, show the keystroke overlay you turn on, and play the key sounds you turn on. They are handled in memory and are never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your settings stay on your Mac in the app's own storage. Passwords you save for a "Type Password" shortcut are kept in your macOS Keychain.
  • Sound folders you choose are read only on your Mac, through access you grant in the file picker.

Deleting the app removes its settings. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
